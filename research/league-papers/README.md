# Surrey Cross Country League papers (Steve's ring binder)

- `1979-09-18-shippen-letter.jpg`: covering letter from Hon. Secretary Clive Shippen, 18 Sept 1979, sending clubs
  the league history he had researched ("no records have been kept by the League since its inception in 1962").
- `1967-68-final-tables.jpg`: 1967/68 final positions. Belgrave 561½, Hercules Wimbledon 866½, Mitcham 951,
  Walton 1180, Ranelagh 1199, Croydon 1338, Surrey AC 1359; individual Gerry North (Belgrave) 9½ pts (Mitchell Cup).
  Every club total equals the sum of our three 1967/68 race scores (Wimbledon 28 Oct 1967, Morden Park
  30 Dec 1967, Petersham 24 Feb 1968), confirming only three races counted: the Esher fixture was cancelled
  because of snow (the binder papers, per Steve).
- Handwritten notes in the binder (not photographed) give places for Rider, Barker, Rowland and Bate in the three
  1967/68 races. They don't fit the senior race (e.g. senior 22nd at Petersham was M Gregory, Walton); Rowland and
  Bate look like Ranelagh juniors (county champs 1969: G Rowland, T Canadine-Bate), so probably a youths' race.
