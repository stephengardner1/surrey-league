## 1983/84 match 4 (Coulsdon): date
We hold 4 February 1984. Athletics Weekly, 24 March 1984, p46 prints the Division One result under "February 18th" (the
same page carries the race photo now on the site). Not changed; worth checking against a second source.

## 1978/79 men, match 4 (Coulsdon, 17 Feb 1979) — team scores added 19 Oct 2026
Match scores were missing. Added from Athletics Weekly 24 Mar 1979 p9 and The Belgravian No. 198 (Apr 1979), which agree:
AFD 215, E&E 279, SLH 308, H/W 316, Walton 375, Belgrave 466, Herne Hill 526 (HHH from The Belgravian only; AW omits it).
The season table had Walton and South London the wrong way round (4th/5th) and Herne Hill on 1807. AW's final placings and the
sum of the four matches give Walton 1472 (4th), SLH 1477 (5th), HHH 1802; fixed. Match 3 date shown as "Jan" corrected to
9 Dec 1978 (The Belgravian No. 198), match 4 to 17 Feb 1979.
