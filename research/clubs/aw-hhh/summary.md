# AW / SCAA trawl: Herne Hill Harriers portraits

**What I covered**
- I ran a text search for the target names across about 800 Athletics Weekly (AW) winter issues from 1974 to 2005, using the reused text files.
- I downloaded about 45 issues to raw/. I looked at about 40 candidate pages as low-resolution overviews, and checked the photo pages at 200–500 dpi.
- I pulled the Surrey County XC Champs senior men results for 1975–2019 from surreyathletics.uk (raw/scaa/) to get first names.

**Counts:** 3 photos (photos.jsonl) and 12 findings (8 fullname, 1 spelling, 3 note).

## Name findings
- **D Glassborow** is **David (Dave) Glassborow**. AW 1984 calls him a former world age-16 1500m record holder.
- **M McEvilly** is **Martin McEvilly** (Surrey Champs 1978–80, Herne Hill; earlier Shaftesbury).
- **R Payne** is **Bob (Robert/Rob) Payne**. He was an RAF corporal and won the Herne Hill 10 in 1986. His 1982 marathon time was 2:30:06.
- **P Mayo** is **Pete Mayo** (medium confidence). He is a different runner from Chris Mayo (C Mayo, Herne Hill, 1990s).
- **B Brown** may be **Basil Brown**, who was Herne Hill in AW 1995–97 and is called "Jamaican Marathon champion" in 1997. Low confidence: the county champs only ever print "B Brown".
- **M Boyle** is **Mike Boyle**, not Mark. AW 2001 says he was 41 in December 2001 and a 2:20 marathoner.
- **Iain Lockett:** the county champs spell it "Iain".
- **Ian Munt** and **Dave Robinson** confirmed. Robinson ran for Collingwood AC in 1992, then Herne Hill from 1993.
- **Henderson:** the county champs show **Jonathan Henderson** (Herne Hill 2009–11). Check that our Richard and Jonny Henderson records aren't mixed up.
- **Mick Harran** (father) and **Tony Harran** (son) both appear in the county champs, separately.

## Problems
- Photos of these runners are rare in AW's 1990s–2000s results pages. Most text hits are results lists with no pictures.
- The AW text is unreliable for spotting captions in later issues, so the photo search was only partial.

**Lead for later:** hernehillharriers.org has news posts and a "125 years" article that may include photos of Alan Barnes and other post-2005 runners (found by web search, not checked).
