# Boxhill Racers in The Belgravian: photo search

## What was searched
- Issues 213, 214, 216, 217, 218, 219, 220, 221, 222, 223 and 224 (1989 to 1996). On request, also 225 to 228 (late 1990s), to look for Simon Fairbrother.
- Every page was rendered at 50 dpi and the photo pages were picked out automatically (about 330 pages, plus 175 in issues 225 to 228). I looked at all of them on contact sheets and rendered anything promising at 200 to 400 dpi.
- I searched the OCR text for Boxhill, Rayner, Fairbrother and Penney/Penny, including near captions.

## Result
I found only **one photo with a Boxhill Racers vest**: issue 218 (May 1992), page 36 of the PDF. It shows the Nower, Dorking, Surrey League match 3 on 18 Jan 1992.
The brief said page 37, but the photo is on PDF page 36, which is also the printed page number.

The caption names only Stuart Paton (71) and Andy Paton (72). The race report on the same page names no Boxhill runner. The photo was taken in the opening charge up the hill, so finishing order can't tell us who they are.
- Boxhill finishers that day (data.json): Ken Penney 4, E Mannion 5, K Howard 13, D Morris 14, M Howard 15, J Weightman 21, S Fry 23, A Robinson 27, S Fairbrother 36, R Tweddle 39, S Bishop 77, E Fallon 91, N Stevens 93, R Brown 97.
- Bib numbers are reissued at each match, so they can't be matched across issues. Andy Paton wears 72 here, but appears to wear 69 at Coulsdon (issue 218, page 38).
- What they look like: 126 is tall, with very short receding hair. 131 has dark curly hair and a slighter build.

## Priority runners
- **Simon Rayner, Simon Fairbrother, Ken Penney:** I found no photo of any of them, in any vest, in issues 213 to 228. All three appear only in results and race-report text, never in a caption.
- **Text mentions worth noting:**
  - 214, pages 38 to 41: Rayner and Penney go 1-2 in the 1989/90 League races. Penney is listed as individual champion and Rayner second.
  - 217, page 31: Bizio "chasing Simon Rayner of Hercules Wimbledon" at the Surrey relays.
  - 218, page 42: "Individual Champions: Seniors: K.Penney (Boxhill) 21 pts".
  - 223, page 15: "Simon Fairbrother took the Hill Billies' back to the front with 9:27" (Belgrave Road Relay, 1995).
  - 225, page 54: Surrey Champs, 14 Dec 1996, mentions Fairbrother and Boxhill's Haywood. The one photo there shows Belgravians Alsop (8) and Beaver (12).
  - 227, page 48: "Boxhill's Simon Fairbrother on his tail" (cross-country report, autumn 1998; the OCR columns are jumbled).
- **No caption anywhere names a Boxhill runner.**

## Leads for bibs 126 and 131
- The original photographer, Ray O'Donoghue, or Boxhill club members could name them from the prints.
- Best guesses are Boxhill scorers who were running near the Patons early on, but nothing in the magazine supports any one name.
