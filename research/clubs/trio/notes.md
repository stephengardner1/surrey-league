# Trio photo search: Rayner, Penney, Fairbrother (searched 17 Oct 2026)

## Simon Fairbrother (Boxhill Racers): 3 photos found, high confidence
- He is the British international 1500m runner, born 28 March 1968 (Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fairbrother). He won the UK 1500m title in 1991, was second in 1993, and took bronze at the 1992 IAAF World Cup in Havana. PBs: 1500m 3:38.64 (Verona, 17 June 1992) and mile 3:56.83 (Gateshead, 1990).
- The Surrey Athletics profile (https://www.surreyathletics.uk/database/profile.php?Y=2026&S=2027&N=613&G=M) runs from Surrey Youths in 1983 through 1995. It lists Boxhill Racers in the 1988–90 county champs and Haringey AC on the track from 1991 to 1995. It shows the same UK Champs 1500m 3:48.59 (Cardiff, 1991) as Wikipedia, which confirms that the Boxhill and international Simon Fairbrother are the same person.
- County titles: 800m 1989 (1:49.7, Boxhill Racers), 1500m 1990 (3:50.2), 1500m 1991 (3:44.7 CBP, Haringey), and 1500m 1992 (3:43.2 CBP). Surrey all-time lists: 1500m 3:38.64, 5th all-time; 800m 1:47.7 (Manchester, 1992), 13th.
- He ran the Surrey XC champs as a senior in 1990, 1991, 1994, 1995, 1996, 1998 and 2000. He was 24th in 1990 for Boxhill Racers.
- Photos: Diomedia/Photoshot (UPPA / Talking Sport) watermarked comp previews from the AAA Champs on 11 June 1994 and the KP Games on 15 July 1995. He wears a Haringey vest in all of them, not Boxhill. The images are under copyright, so use them for private drawing reference only.
- "J Fairbrother" is **Justin Fairbrother**. He ran for Hercules Wimbledon AC in 1989 and 1992, Boxhill Racers/"Boxhill RTC" in 1990 and Haringey AC in 1991. 1500m 3:45.58 (Loughborough, 1991). Profile: https://www.surreyathletics.uk/database/profile.php?Y=2026&S=2027&N=612&G=M. The sources do not say whether he is related to Simon (see 1990 1500m: Simon 1st, Justin 2nd, both Boxhill).

## Ken Penney (Croydon Harriers, then Boxhill Racers): no photo found
- His full name is **Kenneth Penney**, born 26 January 1963 (World Athletics: https://worldathletics.org/athletes/great-britain-ni/kenneth-penney-14352422). PBs: 3000m steeplechase 8:37.70 (Edinburgh, 19 July 1991), mile 3:59.90 (Haringey, 14 July 1991), 3000m 7:59.38 (Crystal Palace, 2 May 1990), and 1500m 3:42.73 (Geneva, 28 July 1990). He also ran 800m 1:50.6 (1991), 5000m 14:21.20 (Watford, 1989, as Boxhill R), and 10,000m 30:30.0 (Melbourne, December 1988).
- He was **Surrey XC champion on 9 December 1989 at Frimley (Boxhill Racers)** and second in 1990 at Happy Valley (39:01). Source: https://www.surreyathletics.uk/crosscountry/champs/champions.php?O=R&A=SM&IT=I
- He won the AAA steeplechase silver in 1991 (8:44.01; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_AAA_Championships) and was a UK Champs steeplechase medallist in 1989 (8:41.87, Jarrow) and 1990 (8:50.90, Cardiff), according to his Surrey profile.
- On the track he ran for Croydon Harriers 1984–88, Boxhill Racers in 1989, and Haringey AC 1990–92, while staying with Boxhill for cross-country. County steeple titles: 1988 (8:52.9 CBP) and 1989 (8:46.2 CBP). He also won the 1989 county 1500m and 5000m.
- Surrey profile: https://www.surreyathletics.uk/database/profile.php?Y=2026&S=2027&N=1501&G=M
- Namesake caution: Andrew Penney is a current Hercules Wimbledon miler (1500m 3:44.19 in 2026, aged 27 in 2018). Nothing found links him to Ken.
- Best lead: Diomedia/Photoshot (UPPA / Talking Sport) covered the 1989–91 UK and AAA Champs and KP Games. A Diomedia search for "Penney" steeplechase from 1989–91 is the most likely way to find a photo, but its search page was blocked here. Getty and Alamy searches were also blocked because they needed approval. Other leads: Athletics Weekly archive scans and Haringey AC histories.
