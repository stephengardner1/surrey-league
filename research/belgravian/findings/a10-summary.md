# a10 summary: B212, B213, B214, B216, B217, B218 (Apr 1988 to May 1992)

317 findings in `findings/a10.jsonl`: 161 result, 55 correction, 29 note, 28 fullname, 16 team, 14 race, 14 photo. I checked the page images for every name, position and time I recorded. 16 photo crops are in `photos/`.

## Answers to the focus questions
- **1988/89 (no Belgrave runners held):** Belgrave were relegated from Div 1 at the end of 1987/88, along with Hercules Wimbledon (B213 p38). They ran the 1988/89 season in **Reebok Surrey League Division II**, so they don't appear in our Div 1 data. I recorded all four Div II matches with full Belgrave lists and times (B213 p43, B214 p36–37):
  - M1, 29 Oct 1988, Wimbledon: Belgrave won with 112.
  - M2, 19 Nov 1988: Belgrave took 1-2-3 and won with 131.
  - M3, 21 Jan 1989, Mitcham Common: Belgrave 2nd.
  - M4, 4 Mar 1989, Waverley: Belgrave 2nd.
  - Final table: H/W 570, Belgrave 692, Croydon 1519. Both top clubs were back in Div 1 in 1989/90.
- **1989/90 Match 4 (essentially missing):** B214 p41 has the full Match 4 (10 Feb 1990, Brockwell Park):
  - Top 3 with times.
  - All 10 team scores, which match ours.
  - 18 Belgrave runners with times.
  - The final overall table and the individual Mitchell Cup and vets awards.
  - B214 also has Match 3 times, which differ by a few seconds from ours, and the cumulative tables.
- **1990/91 (is there a Match 4?):** No. The final fixture, at Happy Valley, was **cancelled because of snow** in February 1991, and the league was decided on three matches (B216 p22). B216 p23 gives the final positions: 1 Boxhill 469 … 6 Belgrave 1406 … 9 Mitcham & Sutton 2213. It also gives full Belgrave lists for M1–M3, which produced several corrections.
- **1987/88 and 1991/92 (initials only):**
  - 1987/88: prose and captions give full names (Justin and Matthew Chaston, Stuart Paton, Simon Maynard, Martin Forrester, Marcello Bizio and others), plus the cumulative standings and the relegation.
  - 1991/92: B218 p34–37 lists every Belgrave runner with times for M2–M4 and the M1 non-scorers. That fixes many garbled names in our data, for example:
    - J Fanny → J Barry
    - J Martin → J Mather
    - M Sizir → M Bizio
    - J Dicont → J Bicourt
    - L Cong → L Coy
    - K Bale → R Bale
  - It also supplies M4 team scores, which we lack. The final 1991/92 table (B218 p42) has Belgrave 5th and records relegations and promotions.
- **Women's league (no extracts held):**
  - 1989/90 senior results: B214 p17.
  - 1990/91 all four matches: B216 p14. Wendy Cotterell was overall Surrey Women's League champion that season.
  - 1991/92: three matches, and Belgrave women were relegated from Div 1 (B218 p22).

## Points to watch
- **Owain Lewes:** B218 p42 says the correct spelling is **Owain Lewes**. My earlier B216/B217 rows suggesting "Owen Lewis" are marked as superseded. Our "D Lewis" and "D Louis" entries are probably him.
- **Venue and date conflicts:**
  - 1987/88: the magazine headings put M3 at Brockwell and M4 at Richmond Park, the opposite of our data. Low confidence, because the President's note contradicts the headings.
  - 1991/92 M3: the magazine gives Dorking (The Nower), 18 Jan 1992. We have Box Hill, 17 Jan.
- **Runners missing from the magazine lists:** a few Belgrave runners we hold are absent, for example 1991/92 M1 186 P Roach (a possible duplicate of P Roche) and 1990/91 M1 179 T Watt. I flagged these at low confidence.

## Problems
- None of the pages was unreadable. B217 (Dec 1991) has only M1 and a brief M2 note. B213 lacks the 1988/89 Div II M3–M4 reports, but B214 covers them. As expected, there is no issue between Mar 1989 and Mar 1991. Young athletes' results were skipped.
