# a13 summary — eHarrier eH01–eH16 and Belgravian B232–B270 (Aug 2004 – May 2009)

All of these issues are born-digital PDFs, and their text layers matched the page images exactly wherever I compared them. I checked images for every key result list. Comparisons used the refreshed `existing/` extracts. There are no `women-*.json` files in `existing/`, so every Belgrave women's League result is recorded as a new `result`, with `found` prefixed "WOMEN:".

**Findings: 422 lines** (results 209, of which women 195 and men 14; fullname 94; correction 37; note 28; race 24; team 22; photo 8)

## What each issue held on the League
- **eH01–eH06, eH08, eH09, eH13–eH16, B232–B235, B237, B241–B249, B252, B255–B260, B264–B267:** no senior League race reports. These are mostly track/summer issues; only fixtures or passing mentions.
- **eH04:** a 2003/04 "results backlog" with full Belgrave lists for men's M1–M4 and women's R1–R4, plus final tables for both.
- **eH07 / eH10 / eH11 / eH12:** 2004/05 men's M1–M4 and women's R1–R4, with full Belgrave lists, team tables and running totals.
- **B236 / B238 / B239 / B240:** 2005/06 M1–M4 (men and women). B240 has the 2005-6 XC Plate table giving full names.
- **B250 / B251 / B253 / B254:** 2006/07 M1–M4 (men and women). B254 has the 2006-07 Plate table with per-League-race columns.
- **B261 / B262 / B263:** 2007/08 M1–M4 (men and women). B263 has the 2007-08 Plate table with SLg1–SLg4 columns.
- **B268 / B269 / B270:** 2008/09 M1–M4 (men and women).

## Problems
- Some printed times are inconsistent in the source itself:
  - 2005/06 women R2: 3rd 18:02 against 2nd 18:20.
  - eH07: R Weston "33:29" (recorded as 23:29).
- B268: the women's R1 prose places (Murphy 7th, Heaton 18th, Hegvold 25th) contradict the results list (11, 24, 31). I used the list.
- No first names were found for P Wytch, W Drey, G Reid, A Bicourt or T St Leger/Ledger. The "Gerry" in "Gerry Adams" is printed in quotes as a nickname.
- No issues were missing or unreadable.
