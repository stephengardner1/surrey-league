## What each issue has on the League
- **B206 (Apr 1983), 1982/83:**
  - Men's matches 2, 3 and 4 Belgrave lists (pp34–35). Match 1 is said to be in Oct 1982 p44.
  - Final Div 1 table: Belgrave 5th with 1529 points, kept their Div 1 place; Croydon and Ranelagh relegated.
  - The season review gives first names: Oliver Foote, Peter Gilbey, Derek Slann, Simon Williamson, Leo Lyons, Adam Christie, Mike Nouch (we hold him as "M Mouch"), Mark Sinclair.
  - Full women's league results for 4 matches, with senior team placings. Final senior position 10th.
- **B207_1983 (Oct 1983):**
  - 1983/84 match 1 in full, with times (p47).
  - Gives "Alistair Morfey" (we hold "A Morfy") and Mike Hayes.
  - New-member profiles: Gary Staines, Kevin Sturman, Andy Leyland, Anton Gayton, Mike Hayes. Photos of Staines and Morfey.
- **B207_1984 (Apr 1984), 1983/84 review:**
  - Belgrave 3rd in Div 1.
  - Full times for all 4 matches. Match 2 adds J Rayner, 141st (not held).
  - Women's league results: Rosemary Honeychurch; final seniors table, Belgrave 12th.
  - Photo of Sturman and Staines sprinting at the Coulsdon league match.
- **B208 (Oct 1984):**
  - 1984/85 match 1 in full. Corrections: R Addington → G Piddington (185th), M Bazire, R Graham, plus S White 145th (not held).
  - Notes that Staines did not finish and that guest second-claim runners ran for other clubs.
- **B209 (Oct 1985), 1984/85 review:**
  - Final table: Belgrave 4th.
  - Corrections: J Bicourt, M Donoghue, M Wilsher, M Head (we hold "R Mead"), D Clack, D Maughan.
  - Full women's league season: Belgrave seniors won match 3 (Honeychurch won individually) and finished 2nd overall.
- **B210 (Oct 1986), 1985/86:**
  - All 4 men's matches; final table: Belgrave 5th, escaped relegation in the last match. Individual championship top 3.
  - Corrections: J Warren for "J Herren"; position offsets in matches 1–3.
  - All 4 women's matches and the final tables.
  - New-members list gives Matthew, Christopher and Julien Chaston, Martin Forrester, Henry Galvan, Stephen Osborne, Trevor Williams and others.
- **B211 (Jun 1987), 1986/87:**
  - Only matches 2 and 4 are printed; matches 1 and 3 are not in any assigned issue.
  - Final table: Belgrave 2nd. Individual champion John Wright — we hold him as "Jean Paul Wright", but the magazine always prints "John".
  - Times for match 2 runners we hold with no time.
  - 3 women's league matches.

## Main issues to flag
- **Two M Lakes.** Martin Lake (first claim) and Mike Lake (second claim) both raced from 1984 to 1987. Most league "M Lake" entries are probably Martin, but I can't confirm it match by match.
- **Conflicting forms of first names:**
  - Francis Ward / Frank Ward.
  - Justin / Julien Chaston.
  - Alistair / Alister Morfey.
  - Debbie Hankins / D.Hawkins (women's team).
- **Results printed twice disagree.** Match 1 of 1983/84 appears in two issues with different positions, and positions also run off in 1985/86 and in 1986/87 match 4. I logged these as low/medium-confidence notes rather than corrections.
- **Match 2 team totals in 1982/83** in the magazine (AFD 327, BEL 772…) are on a different scale from what we hold.
- **Venues:**
  - 1982/83 match 4: magazine says Richmond Park, we hold Petersham.
  - 1984/85 match 2: magazine says Richmond Park, we hold Petersham.
- **Missing results.** 1986/87 men's matches 1 and 3 aren't reported in any assigned issue. 1982/83 men's match 1 is in Oct 1982 (B205), not my set.
