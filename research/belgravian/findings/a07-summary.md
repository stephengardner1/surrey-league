## By issue
- **B193 (Dec 1976).** Report and full list for 1976/77 M1 (p27). Adds first names (Simon Williamson, Paul Roche, Peter Gardner, Trevor Hart, Simon Holmes, Jack Baker, John Baker). Two corrections: B Gorman's time is 31:47, not 31:57; held "J C Baker" is probably J R Baker (Jack R. Baker). Confirms M1 was Division One and that Belgrave won the veterans' race held alongside it. The officers page (p2) and new-members list (pp18–19) provide many first names.
- **B194 (Apr 1977).** Both missing 1976/77 matches, with full Belgrave lists and team scores:
  - **M2:** 27 Nov 1976, Lloyd Park, Croydon. Winner R Pitt 26:17, 153 finished, 28 Belgrave runners.
  - **M3:** **11 Dec 1976** (we hold "Jan"), Brockwell Park. Winner J Roberts 23:24, 144 finished, 24 Belgrave runners.
  - The issue also has the final Division 1 and Division 2 tables for 1976/77 and a list of Division One winners from 1962/63 to 1976/77. Belgrave were relegated for the first time, finishing 7th in all four races.
- **B195 (Oct 1977), B197 (Oct 1978).** Summer issues with no senior League content.
- **B196 (Apr 1978).** All four 1977/78 matches are reported, with full Belgrave lists, winners, team scores and the final table. **These are Division 2 races**, because Belgrave had been relegated. Our extract for 1977/78 holds only Division 1, so these results need a Division 2 home (or a decision to skip them) before import.
  - M1: 22 Oct, Wimbledon.
  - M2: 26 Nov, Petersham.
  - M3: 10 Dec, Crystal Palace.
  - M4: 18 Feb, Milford.
  - Belgrave won Division 2 with 552 points and were promoted.
  - The issue also gives the final Division 1 totals for 1977/78 (AFD 911 … Croydon 1726).
- **B198 (Apr 1979).** Reports all four 1978/79 Division 1 matches.
  - **M1 and M2:** the extracts we refreshed today are heavily OCR-garbled. There are 40 correction lines (32 for M1, 8 for M2), for example "A Blinck" is A Black, "G Voerth" is G North, "P Langster" is R Langheim and "K Samuell" is K Sinclair. Several positions and times also differ.
  - **M3:** two name corrections (T Dowdall and R Kerr).
  - **M4:** 17 Feb 1979, Coulsdon. Winner B Ford 26:43, 11 Belgrave runners, team scores.
  - **Final table:** Belgrave last with 2114 points and relegated again.
  - Prose and the officers page give first names, for example Tony Binda (XC captain), Tom Dowdall, Dennis Brown, Ian Duncan and Allan Callow.
