# a04 summary: B172 to B178 (Dec 1969 to Sep 1972)

## Issues covered
- **B172 (Dec 1969)**: Full results for 1969/70 Match 1 (Wimbledon, 25 Oct 1969) on p43, with the team table on p42. Gives 140 finishers, times for all Belgrave scorers and 33 Belgrave non-scorers. The fixture list on p47 confirms the "North Surrey League" race at Morden Park on 27 Dec 1969 (Match 3).
- **B173 (Mar 1970)**: Season review for 1969/70 on p36–37: final table, the Div II winner and promotion/relegation, and full Belgrave results for Matches 2 (Petersham, 13 Dec), 3 (Morden Park, 27 Dec) and 4 (Coulsdon, Farthing Downs, 21 Feb 1970). Includes finisher counts and a photo of O'Hara and Hart at Coulsdon (p37). Also covers Adrian Major's individual award (p37) and a captain's review (p33).
- **B174 (Jun 1970), B175 (Sep 1970)**: Nothing on the senior League (track and road season only).
- **B176 (Dec 1970)**: Full results for 1970/71 Matches 1 (Wimbledon, 24 Oct), 2 (Lloyd Park, Croydon, 28 Nov) and 3 (Morden Park, 12 Dec), on p22–24. Includes cumulative league standings and a photo of the Match 1 field led by Mann and Kerr (p22).
- **B177 (Jun 1971)**: 1970/71 Match 4 (Coulsdon, 20 Feb 1971) and the final table, on p10. Photos on p38–40 show youths/boys only, so I skipped them.
- **B178 (Sep 1972)**: 1971/72 final table with every club's four match scores, plus full Belgrave results for all four races (p43–44). Includes a photo captioned "Gerry North leads Biscoe and Roberts" (p44). The p48 fixture note covers 1972/73.

## Key corrections
1. **1971/72 Match 3 team scores are attached to the wrong clubs.** According to the final table:
   - Our data has: H/W 242, BEL 244, MIT 276, AFD 284.
   - The magazine gives: H/W 242, AFD 244, BEL 276, RAN 397, CRO 404, SLH 444, MIT 478.
   - **Match 4:** H/W and MIT are swapped. The correct scores are MIT 412 and H/W 494.
2. **1969/70 Match 4:** 40th was D. Trow, not Doug Brew. The Belgrave scoring positions add up to the printed total of 185.
3. **1970/71 Match 4:** T. Hart was 22nd, not 20th. The Belgrave positions add up to the printed total of 160. The magazine also swaps Croydon (353) and Mitcham (362) compared with our data.
4. **1970/71 Match 2:** Thresher's time was 26:54. The 26:06 we hold is the winner's time. Walton and Mitcham are printed as 460 and 470; we hold 461 and 469.
5. **1970/71 Match 1:** Mitcham is printed as 440; we hold 438.
6. **Mike Kortenray's club:** he is shown as Mitcham, not H/W, in 1969/70 Matches 1, 2 and 3.
7. **Match dates:** all 12 races now have exact dates. Matches 3 were in December (27 Dec 1969, 12 Dec 1970, 11 Dec 1971), not January as we hold. The 1969/70 Match 2 was 13 Dec 1969.
