# a06 summary: B186–B192 (Sep 1974 – Aug 1976)

Findings: /home/claude/belgravian/findings/a06.jsonl (256 lines). All League result lists were checked against 300dpi page images.

## What each issue contained on the League
- **B186 (Sep 74)**: nothing on the senior League (out of season). Used only for prose first names.
- **B187 (Dec 74)**: p26 has the **1974/75 Match 1** (26 Oct 1974, Wimbledon): top 3 (P.Adams, B.Ford, L.Presland, all AFD), 30 Belgrave runners with times, and teams AFD 123, CRO 299, H/W 327, BEL 341 (4th). p21 is a youth league race (out of scope). p24 is useful for first names.
- **B188 (Mar 75)**:
  - p8 has the **1974/75 Match 2** (23 Nov 1974, Lloyd Park): top 3 with times, 25 Belgrave runners, 143 finishers, and all 7 team scores (BEL 5th, 438).
  - p8 also has Match 3, which agrees with the archive except for 4–5 tail-end Belgrave names. Examples: the magazine has 101 D.Davies, 115 J.Kelly, 128 R.Hall and 130 C.Manning 39:49. The archive has A Harris, P Holman, D Langley and P Fitton, none of whom are in the magazine.
  - p13 has the **1974/75 Match 4** (15 Feb 1975, Walton): Ford and Roberts, both 31:18; 17 Belgrave runners; all 7 team scores (BEL 6th, 453).
  - p14 has the **final 1974/75 Division 1 table** (BEL 5th, 1,541, "our worst ever"; Herne Hill relegated) and league history.
  - p5: the President warns of possible relegation.
- **B189 (Jul 75)**: nothing on the League.
- **B190 (Nov 75)**: p40 has the 1975/76 Match 1 with a prose report. It gives first names and several corrections:
  - R(not P) Richardson
  - C.Dabbs (archive "C Dages")
  - H.Satchell (archive "H Batchell")
  - N.Kirmatzis (archive "N Kirimatizis")
  - J(not T) Middleton
  - B.Gorman 60th 31:21 (the prose confirms 60th; the archive has 65th)
  - several times differ by 10–60s

  p38 has road-race photos. p8 has a new-members list.
- **B191 (Mar 76)**:
  - p14 is Match 2. It agrees with the archive apart from J.Dudman (archive "J Dadman"), D.Maughan (archive "D Maughn"), G.North 25th (archive 26th) and a few small time differences.
  - p15 is Match 3. It shows K(not R) Duckett, W.Kerr 71st (archive 73rd) and 1–5s time differences.
  - p18 has the **1975/76 Match 4** (21 Feb 1976, Petersham/Richmond Park): R.Holt (H/W) 23:28; 28 Belgrave runners with times; all 7 team scores (BEL 6th, 466).
  - p18 also has the **final 1975/76 table** (AFD 1056, CRO 1086, WAL 1354, H/W 1358, BEL 1464, SLH 1480, RAN 2142; Ranelagh relegated, Herne Hill promoted).
  - Cover photos and photos on p13 and p17.
- **B192 (Aug 76)**: nothing on the League (summer track issue). Used only for prose names.
