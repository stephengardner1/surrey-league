## Problems and open questions
- **1966/67 match scores don't add up to the final table.** Belgrave's per-match scores (374+256+266+161) total 1057, but the table says 1023. H/W, CRO and HHH are also off by a little. The printed Belgrave positions for Matches 2 and 3 sum to 269, not the stated 256 and 266. The narrative suggests Match 2 may really have been about 226. This needs a second source.
- **1965/66 Match 1 Belgrave runners have no overall positions.** The 40 entries carry times only, so they're logged as medium/high-confidence results without a position.
- No pages were unreadable and no issues were missing. The OCR was badly garbled on B158 and B161, so all figures were taken from the page images.
