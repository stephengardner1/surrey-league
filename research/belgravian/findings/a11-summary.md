## Issues
- **B219 (Nov 1992)**: track-season issue. Has no senior Surrey League cross-country content. (p50 "Surrey League title" refers to the women's Surrey *road* series, so it is out of scope.)
- **B220 (Spring 1993)**: full 1992/93 men's Div 1 coverage on pp34–37 (all 4 matches, Belgrave lists with times, Div 1 final table) and women's Div 2 coverage on pp10–11 (all 4 races with Belgrave positions/times, Div 2 final table, promotion). Also has the colour back-page photos (p40) and a Staines photo at Coulsdon (p37).
- **B221 (Winter 1993/94)**: explains the 1993/94 gap. Belgrave men were **relegated to Division 2** after finishing 9th of 9 in 1992/93. The issue covers Div 2 Race 1 (Epsom Downs, 16 Oct 1993) and Race 2 (Ockham Common, 20 Nov 1993) in full, plus women's Div 1 Races 1–2 (Wimbledon 23 Oct, Brockwell Pk 13 Nov 1993).
- **B222 (Feb 1995)**: men's Div 2 Races 3–4 1993/94 (Lloyd Park 8 Jan, Wimbledon 12 Feb 1994) and the Div 2 final table (Belgrave champions, 690, promoted). Also 1994/95 men's Div 1 Matches 1–3 with team scores, women's 1993/94 Races 3–4 with the final table (Belgrave Div 1 **champions**), and women's 1994/95 Races 1–2.
- **B223 (Nov 1995)**: 1994/95 men's Match 4 (Coulsdon), the 1994/95 men's and women's final tables, and women's Races 3–4 1994/95 (prose only).
- **B224 (Jun 1996)**: all four 1995/96 men's matches with full Belgrave lists and times, the men's final table, all four women's races, and the women's final table.

## Key points
- 1993/94: our archive holds only Div 1. Belgrave ran **Div 2** and won it: they won 3 of 4 races and lost Race 3 to Croydon by 21 points. Every Belgrave Div 2 result (4 races) is recorded as a new result.
- 1992/93 Match 3: our Belgrave positions from about 94th onward are **one place too low** compared with the magazine (A Paton 95 not 94, Harding 103, Anderson 113, ... Lawton 174). "59 M Blake" should be **Mike Lake**. The team score is printed as 617 (we hold 627).
