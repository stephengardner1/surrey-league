# a03 summary: B164–B171 (Dec 1967 – Sep 1969)

**227 findings:** result 96, fullname 53, correction 51, note 13, race 9, photo 5. I checked page images for every name, position and time in the lists.

## Key answer: 1967/68 had no Match 4
- Only three senior races were held in 1967/68. Race No.2 (Walton, Esher) was **abandoned because of frost and a blizzard** (B165 p23).
- The three races were:
  - Race 1: Wimbledon, 28 Oct 1967
  - Race 3: Mitcham at Morden Park, **30 Dec 1967**
  - Race 4: Ranelagh at Richmond Park, **24 Feb 1968**
- How this maps onto the archive:
  - Archive "Match 2 (Nov, Morden Park)" is Race 3 on 30 Dec.
  - Archive "Match 3 (Jan, Petersham)" is really Race 4 at Richmond Park on 24 Feb.
- The archive's Match 3 top 10 and Belgrave list are a **copy of Match 2**. The real Race 4 result (Thresher 28:54, Geoff North 2nd 29:06, and so on; 100 finishers, 25 Belgrave) is recorded as correction and result lines. Its team scores match what we hold.
- Final table: BEL 561½, H/W 866½, MIT 951, WAL 1180, RAN 1199, CRO 1338, SUR 1359. Gerry North won the individual title. Surrey AC relegated.
