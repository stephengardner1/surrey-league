# a08 summary: B199-B205 (Oct 1979 - Oct 1982)

The findings are in `a08.jsonl`: 296 lines, all from this agent. All results and positions were checked against the page images.

## The main answer: Belgrave were in Division II
- **1979/80**: Belgrave were relegated from Div I in spring 1979 (B199 p3; B200 p37). In 1979/80 they ran in **Division II and won it**. The final table was Belgrave 864, Croydon 938, Thames H&H 1626, then 7 other clubs (10 teams).
- **1980/81**: Belgrave were back in Div I and finished 7th of 7 (1769), so they were relegated again. SLH went down with them.
- **1981/82**: Belgrave were in **Division II and finished runners-up to SLH** (600 v 768), so they were promoted. There were only 3 races, because the Waverley fixture was cancelled for snow. 12 clubs took part.
- **1982/83**: Belgrave were in Div I. B204 p7 says that from 1983/84 the league grows to 3 divisions (7, 7 and 8 teams).

The archive's 1979/80 and 1981/82 data is Division I only. None of the Div II races are in the archive, so the Div II results below are all new.

## What each issue contained
- **B199 (Oct 1979)**: Editorial note on the spring 1979 relegation to Div 2. No League results.
- **B200 (Apr 1980)**: All 4 Div II matches for 1979/80 (pages 38-40):
  - M1: 20 Oct, Wimbledon
  - M2: 24 Nov, Lloyd Park
  - M3: 8 Dec, Crystal Palace
  - M4: 15 Mar 1980, Mitcham Common
  - It has the full Belgrave lists with times, the winners, the top-3 team scores, the final table and the individual standings (O'Hara 3rd). There is also a good photo of M3.
- **B201 (Oct 1980)**: Nothing on the League. Useful for first names only.
- **B202 (Apr 1981)**: All 4 Div I matches for 1980/81, plus the final table. These match the archive except for the corrections below. Also has women's Surrey League M1 and M2 for 1980/81.
- **B203 (Oct 1981)**: Notes only: the M2 fixture moved to 28 Nov at Richmond Park, and Belgrave hosted the 17 Oct meeting.
- **B204 (Apr 1982)**: Div II 1981/82:
  - M1: 17 Oct, Wimbledon
  - M2: 28 Nov, Richmond Park
  - M4: 20 Feb, Coulsdon
  - It has the full Belgrave lists, team tables and final table. Also women's league results for 1981/82 and the note on the league restructure.
- **B205 (Oct 1982)**: 1982/83 M1 Div I list. The team placings match the archive.
