# a12 summary: Belgravian B225 to B231 (Feb 1997 to Dec 2003)

All 7 issues were read. I checked league results against the page images at 130 to 250 dpi. Findings: `findings/a12.jsonl`, 229 lines.

**Counts by type:** fullname 73 · correction 53 · team 37 · race 27 · photo 18 · note 16 · result 5

## What each issue contained on the League

| Issue | Seasons covered | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| B225 (Feb 1997) | 1996/97 M1 to M3 | Full Belgrave lists, team tables and prose with first names. Women's Div 1 races 1 and 2 (Reigate, Petersham). "25 years ago" 1971/72 final Div 1 table. |
| B226 (Mar 1998) | 1996/97 M4; 1997/98 M1 to M4 | 1996/97 M4 and the final 1996/97 table. Every 1997/98 men's match, with full M2 times (not held) and the final 1997/98 table. Jim Estall was overall individual League winner (Mitchell Cup). Women: 1996/97 last race; 1997/98 R1, R3 and R4 (relegated to Div 2). 1972/73 reprint. Colour photo montage. |
| B227 (Mar 1999) | 1998/99 M1 to M4 | All four matches with cumulative tables, final Div 1 table, promoted clubs and individual winners. Women's Div 2 R1 to R4: Belgrave won Div 2 and were promoted. |
| B228 (May 2000) | 1999/00 M1 to M4 | All four matches. Belgrave won Div 1 for the first time in 28 years (800 to 834), with individual winners. Farah (aged 16) was removed from the M1 result. Women's Div 1 R1 to R4 and final table (Belgrave 6th). |
| B229 (Feb 2001) | 2000/01 M1 to M3 | M1 to M3 with full tables. Women's Div 1 R1 to R3. M4 came after this issue went to press. |
| B230 (Jun 2003) | 2000/01 M4; 2001/02; 2002/03 | Results diary. Final 2000/01 table (men 3rd; women 9th, relegated). 2001/02 all men's matches and final table (Belgrave champions, 730 to 736). Women Div 2 2001/02, won and promoted. 2002/03 all men's and women's matches and finals (men 3rd; women 3rd in Div 1, B team 16th). |
| B231 (Dec 2003) | 2003/04 | No League results; the editor deferred them to B232. Cover photo of Sharp and Dent at the 2003/04 M1. AGM line: men 3rd in 2002/03. |

## Key items for the archive

- **Women's League (none held).** Belgrave team positions, scores and named runners exist for nearly every race from 1996/97 to 2002/03, with Div 1 and Div 2 movements:
  - relegated 1997/98
  - promoted 1998/99
  - relegated 2000/01
  - promoted 2001/02
- **Belgrave runners given full first names.**
  - 1996/97: Matt Yates, Roger Alsop, Jim Estall, Dave Beaver, Dave Pamah, Said Chettati, Mike Kazimierski, Marlon Anderson, Mark Anderson (both "M Anderson" in different matches), Darren Mead, Lee Hurst, Tony Barden.
  - 1997/98: Kassa Tadesse, Clive Gilby, Wesley Minett, Lionel Mann.
  - 1998/99: Hassan Raidi, Saul Armanini, Jerome Curtat, Charles Herrington.
  - 1999/00: Mark Miles, Geremew Wolde, Yitbarek Dinku, Alasdair McLean.
  - 2000/01: Fabien Bernard-Gaudin, Richard Hayman, Joachim Wolf, Guy Lavender, Malachi Byansi.
- **Notable corrections.** Many are OCR-style mistakes in the held data:
  - 1996/97 M1: "N Dough" should be Mike Nouch.
  - 1996/97 M3: P Beaver, G Barton and M Kazimoski should be D Beaver, B Barton and M Kazimierski.
  - 1998/99 M2: "S Feisler" should be S Fowler.
  - 1999/00 M4: "L Hunt", "A Alsop", "D Dehie", "C Leimenacher", "A Lowmeadow" and "N Bale" should be L Hurst, R Alsop, D Ochse, E Lemenager, A Cowmeadow and R Bale.
  - 1999/00 M1: "J Craily" and "D Ogmbe" should be J Galley and D Ochse.
  - 2002/03 M1: "P Ruckley", "R Coulter" and "Steve Kennefick" should be P Buckley, R Poulter and S (Shane) Kennefick.
  - 2002/03 M4: "Jason Webb" should be M Webb. Magazine times are available for every Belgrave runner in this match.
- **Wrong team tables held.**
  - 2000/01 M1 (Belgrave 3rd with 303, not 2nd), M3 (no BOH 266) and M4 (THH won; BOH did not score 105).
  - 2001/02 M1 and M3 differ from the magazine.
- **Probable mis-labelled first names in the archive:**
  - "William Lynch" should be Warren Lynch.
  - "Les Greatorex" should be Lee Greatorex.
  - "Steve Connor" should be Sean Connor.
  - "Lawrence Hurst" and "Liam Hurst" should be Lee Hurst.
  - "Moses Byansi" should be Malachi Byansi.
  - "Andrew Zawadski" should be Adam Zawadski.
  - 2001/02 M4 runner-up: the magazine says **Allen** Graffin, not Andy.

## Problems

- B231 has no League results; 2003/04 is in B232, which is outside my range.
- B230 gives results only as a dense diary, with no prose or names beyond initials for most runners.
- The 1998/99 M4 Belgrave positions in the magazine are offset by about 1 from its own team brackets. I could not reconcile several held names there (G Morgan, D High, R Pitt, M Collett); see the note entry.
- Held 2002/03 M1 "34 J Estall" is not in the magazine's list (low confidence).
