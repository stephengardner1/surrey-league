# a05 summary: B179–B185 (Dec 1972 – Jun 1974)

All 7 issues were read. Every recorded result was checked against the page image. Comparisons use the refreshed `existing/` extracts.

## League content by issue
- **B179 (Dec 72)**, pp38–39: "Surrey C.C. League (Div. 1)". Full Belgrave lists **with times** for 1972/73 M1 (Oct 21, Wimbledon, 171 finished) and M2 (Nov 25, Lloyd Park, 130 finished), plus team scores. Standings after 2 matches: BEL 406, AFD 432. Roll of league champions 1962/63–1971/72.
- **B180 (Mar 73)**, p35: Belgrave lists **with times** for M3 and M4, plus the final 1972/73 table: AFD 863, BEL 875, H/W 1464, SLH 1466, CRO 1481, HHH 1793, RAN 1998. **M3 was Dec 9th 1972 at STREATHAM COMMON, won by L.Presland in 22:55.** Our data has "21st January 1973, Wimbledon Common" and 27:16, both wrong. Also p22 (title lost after 7 years), p54 (new-member list used for full names), p36 (R.Richardson listed as **second-claim**).
- **B181 (Jun 73) and B182 (Sep 73)**: nothing on the senior league (summer issues). Photos only.
- **B183 (Dec 73)**, p29: **1973/74 M1, Oct 20th 1973, Wimbledon Common.** Top 3: Holt H/W 23:29, Briscoe H/W 23:34, Adams AFD 23:41. 35 Belgrave finishers with times; Bicourt 5th was the first Belgravian. Teams: AFD 176, BEL 193, H/W 195, HHH 412, SLH 449, CRO 481, Met Police 576. p5: Charlie Manning broke his ankle in that race.
- **B184 (Mar 74)**, p25: **1973/74 M2–M4 in full**, plus the final table and individual trophy.
  - M2, Nov 24th 1973, Croydon: Holt 25:38; BEL 3rd on 320; 19 Belgravians.
  - M3, **Dec 8th 1973 at Hayes, Kent**: our data says "Jan". Holt 23:43; BEL 5th on 405; 14 Belgravians.
  - M4, Feb 16th 1974, Coulsdon: Ford 26:47; BEL 5th on 363; 10 Belgravians.
  - Final: AFD 776, H/W 985, BEL 1281, CRO 1562, SLH 1620, HHH 1691, Met Police 2025.
  - Individual trophy: Holt 9 pts, Adams 13, G.North 5th on 40.
- **B185 (Jun 74)**: nothing on the senior league. p12 shows Chris Barber is a 2nd-claim member.
