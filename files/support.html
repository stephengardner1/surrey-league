Help

Questions, corrections or ideas? Email hello@slarchive.org and we'll get back to you.

I'm not getting notifications

Check that notifications are on for Surrey League in your iPhone's Settings, and that a Focus mode (such as Sleep) isn't silencing them. Then open the app's Following screen and make sure you're following a league, a club or a runner. Results notifications arrive when provisional results are published, which can be a while after the race.

A result looks wrong

Results, especially older ones, were typed up from photocopies and handwritten sheets, so mistakes creep in. Email us with the race, the runner and what it should say, and we'll fix it. If it changes a current season's result, followers get a correction notification.

I'd like my name removed

Email us with the race and the name, and we'll take it out.
