Privacy

Surrey League Compendium app and slarchive.org · Last updated 21 November 2026

The Surrey League Compendium app and slarchive.org are an independent archive of Surrey Cross Country League results, run by Stephen York. The Android app is published on Google Play by Lara App Limited. This page explains what information they use and why.

The short version: there are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking. The app keeps only what it needs to send you the notifications you ask for.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, your phone gives the app a push token: a random code that lets Apple (on iPhone) or Google (on Android) deliver notifications to that device. We store that token together with what you've chosen to follow (the men's or women's league, clubs and runners) so we can send you the right notifications. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and the token isn't linked to who you are.

Visitor statistics

The website, and the website pages shown inside the app, use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It uses no cookies, doesn't build a profile of you and doesn't follow you to other sites. We also count how often the share images are downloaded, without recording who downloaded them.

Where it's kept

Follow choices and push tokens are kept on Cloudflare's servers, which run our notification service, and notifications are delivered by Apple's push notification service on iPhone and by Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android. We don't sell or share this information, and we don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you stop following everything, turn off notifications or delete the app. Push tokens that Apple or Google tells us are no longer valid are deleted automatically.

Your choices

  • Change what you follow at any time on the app's Following screen.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone's Settings, or delete the app, to stop them.
  • To ask us to delete your stored follow choices, email hello@slarchive.org.

Deleting your data

The Surrey League Compendium app has no accounts. The only data we hold about you is your phone's push token and the leagues, clubs and runners you follow, and only if you turned notifications on.

  • To delete it yourself: switch off everything on the app's Following screen, or turn off notifications for the app in your phone's Settings. Your token and follow choices are then removed from our records.
  • To ask us to delete it: email hello@slarchive.org and tell us which leagues, clubs and runners you follow, so we can find your record. We delete it within 30 days.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Anonymous page-view counts hold no personal data and cannot be linked to you.

On your phone

The archive and your settings are stored on your phone so the app works offline. They are removed if you delete the app.

FantaXC

The app's FantaXC tab opens the FantaXC fantasy game, which is run separately by its creator at fantaxc.com. On Android it opens in your phone's browser. If you sign in or pick a team there, FantaXC's own terms and privacy information apply.

Results

The archive contains names, clubs and finishing positions from published league results. If you're a runner and would like a result corrected or your name removed, email hello@slarchive.org.

Children

The app isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
